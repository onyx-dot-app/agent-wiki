# AI Wiki Helper

The chat panel built into every page — a teammate that has already read every wiki page you have access to.

Click the bubble in the bottom-right to open it. Expand it using the diagonal arrows in the widget for more room. Your conversation and history are preserved and accessible later.

---

## What it can help with

- **🔍 Answer questions about the wiki.** *"What did we decide about pricing?"* — it searches, reads the relevant pages, and cites them.
- **✏️ Edit pages for you.** *"Add a Risks section to the launch plan detailing..."* Changes go through the normal commit flow, attributed to the helper, with full history.
- **⚡ Create and update triggers.** *"Create a trigger to notify me whenever the migration project status changes."* It writes the trigger and tells you when it fires.
- **🧭 Explain the product.** Ask how a feature works or where something lives in the UI and it will walk you through it.

> If your admin has enabled **web search**, the helper can also pull information from the internet — useful both when drafting new pages and when answering questions that need fresh outside context.

Edits respect your permissions: if you can't write to a page, the helper can't either on your behalf.
